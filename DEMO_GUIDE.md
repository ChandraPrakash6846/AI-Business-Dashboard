# AI Business Dashboard - Video Demo Script & Outline 📹

This document provides a 3-5 minute demonstration script and video outline for presenting the **AI Business Dashboard** project.

---

## ⏱️ Video Demo Overview (3 - 5 Minutes)

| Timestamp | Section | Visual Actions & Key Talk Tracks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:45** | **Introduction & Problem Statement** | • Screen shows `PRESENTATION.html` slide 1.<br/>• *"Hello! Welcome to the demonstration of the AI Business Dashboard."*<br/>• Explain the goal: turning raw datasets into instant interactive insights and natural language queries. |
| **0:45 - 1:30** | **Data Ingestion & Auto-Cleaning** | • Switch to Streamlit dashboard (`http://localhost:8501`).<br/>• Show the built-in sample dataset or upload a CSV/Excel file.<br/>• Point to the **Header Banner** and **Data Health Check**: *"Notice how the system automatically cleans duplicates, handles missing values, and calculates KPIs."* |
| **1:30 - 2:30** | **Interactive Visualizations & Anomaly Detection** | • Navigate to **📈 Interactive Visualizations** tab.<br/>• Change chart types (Bar, Line, Donut, Scatter).<br/>• Click **🔍 Advanced Analytics** tab -> Click **Run Isolation Forest Anomaly Scan**.<br/>• Highlight the correlation matrix and flagged outlier transactions. |
| **2:30 - 3:30** | **Natural Language Queries** | • Click **🤖 Natural Language Queries** tab.<br/>• Click quick sample button *"Total sales by category"* or type *"Profit by region"*.<br/>• Show how the assistant parses plain text into aggregated data and plots custom Plotly charts. |
| **3:30 - 4:30** | **Exporting Reports & History** | • Click **📥 Download PDF Report** and open the generated PDF.<br/>• Click **📥 Export Excel Report** and highlight the multi-tab formatted workbook.<br/>• Open **📜 Analysis History** to demonstrate SQLite session logging. |

## 🎬 Tips for Recording

1. **Resolution & Audio:** Record screen at 1080p full screen with clear microphone audio.
2. **Key Shortcut:** Use arrow keys (`->`) to transition through `PRESENTATION.html` slides smoothly.
3. **Streamlit Command:** Ensure app is running via `streamlit run app.py`.
